Open-source contribution and reproducible science are extremely important. I maintain several personal research projects focusing on graph neural networks, link prediction, and network visualization.

### Relevant Open-Source Projects

- **fm4tlp:** A foundational package offering transfer learning pipelines specifically designed for temporal link prediction in dynamic graphs.
- **ILP (Inductive Link Prediction):** Implementing novel inductive approaches to predict edges for unobserved queries and temporal nodes.
- **AI-Bind Implementation:** Assisting AI-driven protein engineering workflows, specifically targeting protein-ligand interactions.
- **MultiViz:** A plugin for Gephi to visualize multilayer and complex networks easily.

Feel free to browse through my [GitHub](https://github.com/ChatterjeeAyan) for a comprehensive look at the various libraries and data scripts I've built.
