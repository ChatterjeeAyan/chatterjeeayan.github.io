I'm the founder and CEO of [BioClarity AI](https://www.bioclarity.ai/), where we combine machine learning with biological data to accelerate gene therapy and editing research. I hold deep expertise in building AI tools for computational biology and also consult on AI for gene therapy with Alexion AstraZeneca Rare Disease.

### Areas of expertise

* Network & graph machine learning (link prediction, embeddings, GNNs)
* Computational biology & AI for drug discovery
* Applied AI in rare diseases and gene therapy
* Open science & reproducible research

Ayan received his Ph.D. from the [Network Science Institute](https://www.networkscienceinstitute.org/) at [Northeastern University](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/) under the supervision of Professor [Tina Eliassi-Rad](http://eliassi.org/). He is interested in graph machine learning, specifically link prediction, graph embeddings, applying network science in graph machine learning, and biological networks. He got his Bachelor of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering from [Jadavpur University](http://www.jaduniv.edu.in/), India, and holds a Master's in Electronic Systems Engineering from the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/). Before joining NetSI, Ayan worked at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/), developing and optimizing GPU architectures for AI and video processing applications.
